The project

Fan restoration

The world of Union χ, back in your hands.

Kingdom Hearts Union χ closed its servers in May 2021. The app still in the stores is only a theater mode: the game itself is gone. Re-Union X brings it back as a game you can play on your own computer.

How you can help

Much of the game was downloaded from the servers after install and is now rebuilt by the project. What helps most is knowledge: how screens, events and modes looked and worked. We ask only for information, never for game files.

What we are looking for

  • MOST WANTEDScreenshots and videos of the 36 reconstructed enemy families (Gearbits, Cy-Bugs, Burrfish, Green Requiem, Gummi Saucers... the list is in Status), to check and improve their look, and of the enemies of events that are not in the game yet
  • EVENTSScreenshots, videos, rules and rewards of events, Coliseum, Union Cross and PvP
  • KNOWLEDGEGuides, notes and memories of how a mode or screen worked
  • ANYTHINGYour own screenshots and videos of any screen of the game, any year

Please do not send or post game files (APK, OBB, downloaded data, backups): we cannot accept them.

How to play

Today the setup is long and complex: an Android emulator on Windows, the original client that you must dump from your own device, the server of this project and a few network settings. When the work is finished it will be simplified with a launcher.

This is a work in progress: expect bugs, crashes and screens that are not there yet. No game files are distributed here.

Setup guide

Found a bug?

Report it with the bug form on GitHub. A good report has:

  • What you did, step by step, and what you expected
  • What happened instead (error popup, crash, wrong screen)
  • Where: screen, quest number, medal or banner involved
  • A screenshot or video, if you can
  • The server log around the moment of the problem
  • Your setup: emulator and version, Windows version, project version (commit)
